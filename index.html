Know your rights

The Bill of Rights belongs to every one of us. Here's what it says, in plain language.

Current Rights
§9EqualityYou're equal before the law, and no one may unfairly discriminate against you.§10Human dignityYou have dignity, and the right to have it respected and protected.§22Freedom of trade, occupation and professionAs a citizen, you can choose your own trade, job or profession.§27Health care, food, water and social securityYou have the right to access health care, enough food and water, and social security.

How it works

From a worry on your mind to a petition in Parliament – here's how we make a plan together.

  1. 01

    Share your fear

    Write it, choose who sees it, then check it and post. It takes 3 quick steps, and you can stay anonymous – always.

  2. 02

    We check for a right

    We look for a proposed right that already covers your fear, and show you which rights it touches.

  3. 03

    Talk it through and vote

    Comment, suggest changes and vote up or down.

  4. 04

    Legal review

    Once a proposed bill gets 10 000 upvotes, verified legal agents such as LexiSA, Lexio, Virtual Lawyer SA and LawFinder (Anita AI) review it before it goes to Parliament as a petition.

  5. 05

    Off to Parliament

    We take the proposed rights to a legal team such as Change.org or the Legal Resources Centre, who then take them to the South African Parliament as a petition.
