Proposed bills / Fair Work on Platforms
New proposed bill

Fair Work on Platforms

Platform workers get fair pay, notice and a way to appeal before they are removed.

Sections that may apply: §23 labour relations · §22 freedom of trade, occupation and profession

A community idea, not a change to the law.

Understand

Delivery riders, drivers and freelancers depend on apps that can change pay or remove them overnight. This proposed right asks for basic fairness.

In plain words

  • Pay rules are shown before work is accepted.
  • You get notice and reasons before being removed.
  • You can appeal to a person.
§23

Labour relations

You have the right to fair labour practices, to join a union and to strike.

§22

Freedom of trade, occupation and profession

As a citizen, you can choose your own trade, job or profession.

What this is not

This is not law and not legal advice. Only Parliament can amend the Constitution. If this idea gets enough support it goes to a legal review first.

Review the bill

Draft text for discussion. It will change as people respond.

1. Purpose

To extend fair labour practices to people who earn a living through digital platforms.

2. Clear pay

Platforms must show how pay is worked out before each job is accepted.

3. Fair removal

A worker may be removed only with notice, reasons and a chance to appeal to a person.

This draft text has not been checked by a lawyer.
